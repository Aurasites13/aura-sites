Aura Sites is run by Maximilien, a web designer and developer based in Tokyo, working in English, Japanese and French.

Why Aura Sites exists

Most small businesses in Japan, restaurants especially, are stuck choosing between an expensive agency, a generic template builder, or no real website at all. Aura Sites exists to build the middle option: a custom, handcrafted site, delivered fast, at a fixed price, with professional translation and AI search optimization included rather than sold as a confusing extra.

How we work

Every project starts from a blank canvas, not a template, and goes through the same process regardless of tier: a discovery call, a first look at the design, then a short path to launch. Translation is handled by Asiatis, a translation agency with over 25 years of experience, not machine translation.

Based in Tokyo

Aura Sites is based in Tokyo and works with businesses across Japan, in person where useful and remotely everywhere else.
