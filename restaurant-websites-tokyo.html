English websites for restaurants in Tokyo

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Aura Sites is a Tokyo-based web design studio that builds English and bilingual websites for restaurants and small businesses in Japan, so foreign tourists and customers can find you, read your menu, and get in touch, whether they're searching on Google or asking an AI assistant.

Who builds English websites for restaurants in Tokyo?

Aura Sites does. We're a small studio founded by Maximilien, working directly with restaurant owners and small businesses across Tokyo and Japan to build custom, handcrafted websites, not templates. Every project includes professional English translation, done in partnership with Asiatis, a translation agency with over 25 years of experience.

Do I need an English website for my restaurant in Japan?

If you want foreign tourists and residents to find you, yes. Many visitors search for restaurants in English before they arrive, and an AI assistant asked for a recommendation can only mention your restaurant if it can find and understand information about it online. A Japanese-only site, or no site at all, makes that much harder.

How can my restaurant attract foreign tourists online?

Start with a site that actually answers what a visitor needs: your menu, hours, location, and whether you take reservations, in English as well as Japanese. From there, the same site needs to be easy for search engines and AI assistants to read, which is what AI search optimization (included from the Launch tier up) is for.

How do I get my restaurant recommended by ChatGPT?

No one can guarantee a specific AI recommendation, because AI answers change by person and by day. What actually helps is making your site easy for AI to find, understand, and quote: clear English text (not just a photo of your menu), a current address and hours, and answers to the questions people actually ask. That's the basis of AI search optimization.
