Case study: Asiatis

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Asiatis is a certified translation agency. Aura Sites built their bilingual English and French website, including service pages, a blog and a quote request flow.

Who it was for

Asiatis is a certified translation agency serving businesses, law firms and institutions, with services including certified translation prepared to IRCC requirements.

What was built

A full bilingual site in English and French, with dedicated service pages, a blog, and a quote request flow for new clients. The site is available at asiatis.ca.
